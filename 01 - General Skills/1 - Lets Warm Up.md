## Descripción
If I told you a word started with 0x70 in hexadecimal, what would it start with in ASCII?
## Solución
- Entrar a https://www.rapidtables.com/convert/number/hex-to-ascii.html

- Respuesta: picoCTF{p}

## Solucion 2
- Ir al intérprete de python 
```>>> int(0x70)
112
>>> chr(112)
'p'
>>> 
```
